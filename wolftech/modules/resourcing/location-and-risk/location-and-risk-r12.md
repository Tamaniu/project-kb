## SHOOTS AND LOCATION WORK


### INTRO

As you develop, you are planning it may be important to define where you plan to shoot your story or production.

### SETTING A LOCATION

####### Adding a location

Doing so is easy from the Map section of your story or production.

If you navigate down from the story of the production details section, you will find the map section.

Here you can add a location with the + button.

Search for a location using the google maps integration

Set it as your main location if multiple locations exist

Add a description or notes for your team and even add a start and end time for it if it is tied to an event or a schedule:

Once added, your colleagues will be able to know that a shoot is happening in that location and will be able to collaborate with you.

The location is also available on the mobile phone were clicking on it will open your phone map application to get quick directions to it.

####### Drawing on the map

To make certain information clearer sometimes it is useful to draw on the map. To do so select the draw on map option:

Then use the different tools to add shapes and colors to better define your location map

This will be visible to all team members, including in the mobile app.

####### Points of Interests

Points of interest are places of logistical relevance that can help your team while on location. Think of parking lots, hospitals, transport hubs, disposal facilities or equipment pick up venues, sound studios, etc. Team members can add points of interest from the mobile app or via the web app in the map module.

Once you add a location you will be able to see nearby points of interest and add them to your story or production. They display dimmed down until you add them to your project:

This will make them visible to your team members working on the location.

In the map view you can use the eye icon to filter by the points of interest resource types to only see places that are useful to you:

Note, points of interest can also be used for found shooting locations and location scouting. Simply add a point of interest from the location you found, take pictures, and add them to the profile and add any notes as needed.


####### Adding a Location to a Task (Long form)

Whilst on a story a location will be added to the story. Production can have many locations, and they may be added to multiple tasks.

On the task detail level, add a location and select from the list or add a new one:

### RISK MANAGEMENT

Once you have defined the location you will want to do a risk assessment for it before you can book any team members on the location.

In a story the risk assessment can be found in the assignments section as it is mandatory for location bookings:

In a Production however a risk assessment will be visible once you add a location to a task and can be found on the map section of the task

Once you add a risk assessment to the location it will be visible in any task that relates to such location.

To fulfil the risk assessment depending on your organization you may just need to select from a list of potential risks, that may be level according to high risk, HSE (Health and Safety) or Insurance risk or you may need to upload a reference document:

Some of these risk items may contain links to safety guidelines:

All of this can be managed by your risk team in the admin section.

Once you add the risk assessment, you can book people to the location or task.

For high-Risk productions where support may be required ahead of the shoot you can file a guidance request for risk management support in the logistic tab where you could add risk mitigation items if required and discuss it with the team ahead of filing the risk assessment

Note your Risk Assessment team can leverage the groups function to add certain projects to a high-risk group to closely monitor them

In the map view they will also be able to see all stories and productions happening across the globe and be on the lookout for conflicting areas

And if Dataminr is part of your account you can use it for the most advanced early event detection to better understand if any unknown risks are present around the proposed location

If a dataminr event is worth relating to a story in such location it can be dragged into the story to link them so the team members can keep track of its progress

Finally alerts can also be used to distribute risk alerts for people in the field. These will be visible on the mobile app.


### MAP OVERVIEW

####### Map searches

In the map view you can search for available resources based on location.

On the right menu you can filter by what you are looking for (Crew, Guest, Equipment, Teams, Points of Interests or Stories-Productions).

With the search bar you can search for a specific location or area.

And with the zoom in buttons, you can zoom in and out to see territory.

The right column can be collapsed with the blue bottom right button.


####### Distance to location

With the map opened if you select a story from the left menu and select a resource from the map you will be able to select the show distance button which will tell you the path, distance, and time it will take that resource to get to the location by car

Assigning from the map

If you are happy to assign the resource based on its location, you can drag the resource card over to the story card from the left menu

####### GPS “Live” Production

Resources who make themselves available via the Wofltech Go app for Live production collaboration will show with a green indicator and if you click on them, they will be clearly displayed as available.


####### Vehicle Tracking

We have several integrations that allow you to track your production vehicles in real time in the map view (See Abax for example)

With this functionality it is easier than ever knowing where your production vehicles are to determine which one is best positioned to cover a story.

If you have a location defined, you can work out its distance and how long it will take them to drive to the required location.

If the vehicles are showing live GPS data, you will be able to see their position updating in real time for improved coordination.

### RESOURCING

####### Resourcing Crew

Once the risk assessment is out of the way you can resource people and equipment to such a location. For location resourcing it may be easier to open the location in map view.

Once in map view you can check nearby stories, freelancers, guests, or points of interests

Click on any of them to work out the distance and the time it will take them to get to the location

If you are OK with it, you can easily drag the card to the story left menu to resource them

Note, this is not currently possible with productions

The same function can be used to find equipment or guests or points of interest on the map and resource them to a story.


####### Resourcing Shooting Equipment

Equipment may be requested in advance in the story or production.

Most teams will request the main camera and, in that request, include all the information of the other elements they may require:

Those managing Equipment request can be notified and check for incoming camera requests (left panel)

Then click to see camera availability, assign it, and draw the right panel to add accessories as per the request, like the haivision transmitter below:

In the request they can have conversations with the requester to discuss requirements or confirm assignments

### CONNECTED DEVICES

####### Connected Cameras


Wolftech can orchestrate workflows to synchronize your cameras or transmitters on location to your story-production and its Media Asset Management.

Wolftech supports these workflows today with

- SonyXDCam via SonyC3p

- LiveU

- Soon with Haivision Units


If you book one of these units and they are configured correctly in the admin section, you will be able to see them in the LIVE Media Section


Depending on the units you will be able to

- See a thumbnail preview of what is being recorded

- See its online status

- Check the device battery remaining

- Access the device location


The camera operator will be able to choose the story-production they are shooting for, and the media will either

- Land on the Story Collection directly

- Display first in the Footage section from where it can be manually selected and ingested into the story Media Collection

####### Vehicle Tracking

Note - Vehicle tracking via GPS is possible through integration. Ask your admin for more information. Apps like Abax and others are supported. Depending on the app this may include battery left on electric vehicles as well as distance to destination and of course your templated fields that may include, brand, model, number plate, etc.


### MOBILE APP

####### Check daily assignments

Working on location is where the mobile application shines.

As you prepare to go to your shoot you will be able to see your assignments for the day:

Note how in certain instances you will be asked to confirm whether you can take care of a task. Simply accept or reject it depending on your availability.

Once you have the correct bookings in your daily assignments list, open it to see your booking and have a conversation about the task assigned


####### Communicate about assignments

Use the message function to talk with the requester about the task


####### Find additional information about the story

Navigate to the details page by clicking on the title of the story or production to find all the information about the story itself and use all the functions you are used to on the web:

####### Location Overview

Check the story and its bookings on the location to reveal all the location information:

####### Risk Assessment

Clicking on the risk assessment will reveal the link to the risk guidance or the document uploaded.

####### Resource Profiles

Clicking on a team member profile will reveal their details and allow you to contact them or get to their location

Clicking on the location or a point of interest will reveal its location and allow you to trigger your mobile default maps application to get directions to it

Clicking on an equipment profile may reveal operational guides on how to use the camera and other important information for getting the carnet permissions sorted if needed.

####### Contributing Media

To upload media to the story from location navigate to the board of the story and use the take picture function or upload photo and select from your camera roll:


####### Adding Points of interest

Adding more points of interests is easy from the map menu in the mobile app

Then it is a matter of searching the place you want to add

And once you find it, add it

Add the details in its profile including the type of point of interest it is and then it will be available to others

####### Alerts

Alerts are supported on the mobile app so journalists on the field can find alerts based on feeds or internal alerts and then make stories out of them.

### ADMIN

####### Map Settings

To enable Mapping workflows, you will need to enter your Google Maps API Key in the admin external connections section:


There you will also be able to set your preferred map language, the center point for the map module as well as the different zoom levels for when the map is shown in the application.


####### Risk Assessment Admin


To set your risk assessment go to the admin risk assessment section

Here you can define your risks and add links to guidance


Alternatively don´t add the options if you´d like people to provide a document instead. A link to the document could be added in the useful links area.


To enable risk assessment guidance requests to do so in the admin guidance section:


You will need to make sure a resource type for your risk management team exists first.


To add groups for risk assessment monitoring simply do so in the left menu group section


To setup a template for your risk alerts do so in the admin alerts section


For more information on dataminr usage for early event detection contact your administrator

####### Mobile admin

The mobile app can be accessed via the My Page section.

After you download the app, you can scan a QR code to link the application to your wolftech web instance.


To receive notifications, make sure to enable notifications and location sharing for the mobile app.


You will also have the possibility to share your GPS location via the mobile app. This defaults to NOT share it. Each user will be able to activate it and deactivate them as needed in their profile section


There you will also be able to set your preferred options for your profile, your board, notifications, or language preferences.


####### Equipment Request Settings

To set up your equipment add your relevant resource types in the admin section and define whether the resource type can be requested

Define the profile template to make sure you collate the right information about your equipment. We recommend doing most of this at the very top level, so the metadata is consistent and searchable

Some of these fields can be made searchable and filterable, which is a great way of managing your equipment.


Then define the request template. In most circumstances all shooting equipment questions are added around the camera.


Note how you can make certain fields mandatory with the required check box.

Do select to show on main tab if you want the request form to be visible on the main tab or don´t select it if you would like to have a secondary tab on the request for this information.


Connected Devices


For Connected Camera workflows you will need to setup the resource with the right workflow

In the admin section select the camera workflow and connect your resource to the correct transmitter profile.
