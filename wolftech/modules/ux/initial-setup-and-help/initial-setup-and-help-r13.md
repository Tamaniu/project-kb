## USER INITIAL SETUP AND HELP
### MY PAGE SETUP

As you are welcomed into the platform you will want to set your profile up.
Navigate to the My Page section from the the top right menu:

Here you can update your:
Profile
- Change your profile picture
- Update your preferred name
- Select your default department or brand (It doesn't have much of a consequence if you work across multiples but this will become your shortcut default when creating new productions, you can always change it manually each time)
- Set the office you are currently working from (as opposed to the offices field, which lists the offices you operate in, the currently working from office is your base office)
- Select your default calendar (this is currently not in use but future facing) and you can always view other calendars but it can be the default calendar listing events.
- Add a location so people can find you (this is optional and it can be  your working office address) It will help people understand where you are located when collaborating globally.
- Change your password for accounts not controlled via SSO login.
Settings

Here you can adjust
- the language to your spoken language
- define the clock type you use (12 or 24hs)
- Select your display comfort mode experience (High contrast, wide letter spacing and large target areas)
- Manage private mention groups. These are easy groups that anyone can define to speak to several users at once with and @mention.

Note: Your organization will have a custom language, so the system speaks your operational way of talking. Please do not change the language without confirming with your admin.

Notifications
Set up your notification's preferences (In app, Web, emails, mobile etc.)
Note: Web browser notification will require you to enable them in Chrome.
Some notifications may be enforced by the organisation to always come in a format and always reach all users. Reach out to your administrator for further details.

When are you notified?
- When someone gives you access to a production (freelancers) they are notified of it.
- Confirm a booking to notify your colleague of the assignment. They will know it was you who booked them. If unconfirmed they will also find out right away.
- Mentions: If someone mentions you on a task, production, or booking message board you are notified of the message and who tagged you.
- Guidance and Approvals: If you start a guidance request (R&C-Legal, etc)  or you are booked on it, or you decide to follow it
- Pubpoint Changes notifications are automatically driven by the publishing templates but if you book someone against a deliverable they will be notified of every change of status to such delivery
- Followed Content Changes: If you choose to follow a story or a production you will be notified of its changes.
- All people assigned to the project (inc. key resources) are notified of Greenlight.
- The greenlight process will also send a summary of the information that is typically delivered via email.
- Production Status Notifications (such as when a task starts, completes or is delayed) are easier to manage by role types based on production templates or manual additions on the task itself.
- System Admin Warnings: If your administrator wants to send a warning you can receive that as a notification
Media Board
Here you can add Links to work/passion highlights, or supporting images or documents to your board to make your profile stand out.

Mobile app
In the My Page menu, follow the link to download the app on the left for either iOS or Android devices. Then open up the mobile app and choose to scan the QR code from within the app to link it to our database.
Please be aware that this app is still in development and has not yet been customized for the Production module.

The app is however very useful to
- Search for Employee/Freelance contacts and directly contact them
- See the tasks you are booked on
- Be notified on your phone when someone assigns you a task
- Check Newswires-Feeds directly on the app
- See Risk Assessments for your location
- Get directions to Points of Interests or Locations

ProTip: If you need to reset the QR Code and reconnect to the database go to the hamburger menu on the left and choose About. Then scroll down to the reset option and scan the QR code again
### HOME PAGE  SETUP

The home page is where you can see at a glance all of the most relevant information for your day to day work.

It contains a number of panes with different information
ProTip: Some users may ask if you can reorganise the panes in the home dashboard. This is not possible on the web but it is possible on the mobile app.

Top Stories
Here stories or productions marked as top stories or top episodes are highlighted the top priority stories/projects of the day. It tends to be used less for long form and more for the top news stories of the day although productions can be marked as top episodes and will be displayed here if needed.
Do note this displays all global top stories and episodes for the departments you have access to. This is not the top stories of your personal department so use them carefully.

Department Stories
You can think of this section as a way to be in the loop of what others in your department(s) are creating to see if there is common ground on stories to collaborate or expand, etc.
To select which projects you would like to display in your home Department Stories click the gear icon next to it and select the departments, offices and content types you want to see. Note it will default to your home department so it always shows you content on first login. This setting will be applied both on your web and connected mobile app.

Today/Upcoming Tasks
Any bookings today will show on the Today´s Tasks pane.
Under the today and upcoming tasks, you have a settings menu where you can select if you want to remove any tasks that you mark done from the list (or show completed tasks)
You can also decide to show overdue tasks, this is particularly important with long form where most projects do last more than a day and define how long into the past you would like to show those past tasks.
For upcoming tasks, you can define how long into the future your upcoming tasks should investigate.

- On the stories tasks you can click to navigate to the booking request or the story
- On the episode tasks you can click to navigate to the booking request, the task or to the production
- On stories you can mark them done by checking on the hover over check box

- On episodes you can click on the status icon to update the status of the task directly from the home dashboard.
Note, if you hover over the booking request you can also mark your individual booking as done. This will not mark the task as Done as your task may contain multiple bookings so if you are completing a task it is better to do it from the tasks status menu
- Finally, on the top bar you can also click on the calendar icon to check your personal assignment calendar for the week
My content
This pane will display projects created by you, that you are the owner of or where you have been booked or added as a key resource. Consider this a shortcut to your projects for those managing multiple productions.

### LEFT (QUICK BROWSE) MENU SETUP

The left menu is all about setting your day-to-day work to track the items that you care about. This will define what you see on the web and in the mobile app.

Stories
The second icon on the left below the home icon opens up a view to see all Active stories as well as evergreen projects. By Active we mean projects where their start date is in the past and their end date is in the future.
Also note you can change the guiding date which sits at the top and defaults to today's date. So if you wanted to see yesterday's active projects you could change the date at the top.
To narrow what you see you can create a filter to select the brands you work across, in the filter always check the include productions checkbox and then select the production offices or the type of projects (formats) you want to display. Be sure to sort by Deadline (priority order):

Note productions are not listed here.  If you haven't given dates yet to your project it will continue to show here as a timeless production.

*Note, when you click on a department or brand you will see an “All - None” option pop up to the left of the check mark. Select All for each brand you would like to see all projects in future added to it. You will see the upward arrow icon become orange to the left of the brand to confirm selection.

Event Calendars
A Calendar of Events can be set up to keep people informed about important dates. This may take the form of a news calendar with cultural, political or social events out of which you may create productions or it could be as simple as OOO type of calendars for the teams. Once they are set up you create new events, give them dates and they will show up for the relevant dates. Simple.

External Calendars
External Calendars allow to intake information from 3rd party calendars that you can subscribe to. Out of them you can create stories

Publishing
Here you can track deliveries on the date above. You can filter by the below:

With the eye icon you can also select whether you want to display the publishing time and the staff contacts working on it. The Total run time for the publishing folder will display on top.
. It will always be sorted by Publish time. The icons on the right indicate the status of the delivery. If you choose to select to show pictures you can also display the thumbnail selected (see option in right)

Production Navigation and Structure

On the left menu you can navigate through the departments or brands by clicking on the clapperboard icon to find your production.

The hierarchy follows as follows

- Department - Sub departments (or Brand - subbrands)
- Show
- Season
- Episodes

There is a search bar at the top that allows you to search by Show-department Name (Not production name)

and from there you can drill into the seasons and actual productions.

Clicking on the season will reveal the season, clicking on the episode will reveal the episode. Note there is no “Show Page”. A show is like a department or brand and does not contain information other than serve an organisational purpose.
Useful Links
Useful links allow you to have the go to information for our editorial and production teams to hand without having to navigate out. .
Pins
You can pin any story, production, event, feed or an alert to create a shortcut in your home dashboard. Typically this is done in the 3 dot menu of the asset.

To reveal all pined items click on the pin icon on the lower left corner to display pin items and make it easy for you to return to your favorite content by simply clicking on them.

Note you can also filter the type of pins by item category (Feeds, stories, events, or alerts)

To unpin items use the blue triangle to reveal the unpin button

This whole section can be dragged up and down to reveal more pinned items
