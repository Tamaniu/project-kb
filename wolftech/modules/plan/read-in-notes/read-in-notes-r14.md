## READ-IN NOTES (LOGS)


### INTRO


Read-in Notes are like a sibling to a story or an episode that allows you to gather some information in a text block, add attachments with a board, share it with your teams


This can be used for internal information sharing, morning read-ins and can really help with the onboarding and communication process.


### Quick Add


Read-in notes can be created from the quick add menu


As opposed to Stories, the Description field will be mapped to its Notes Section.


Read-in notes can be private. Simply select the option when you create them. Useful for private briefings until you are ready to share with a department.


### Categorization

Read-in notes are not a content creation project, as such Read-in notes cannot be turned into stories or productions as they are unique. However, a linked story, pitch, event, or read-in note can be created out of them

- A read-in note cannot be shared with other departments, but they can be copied to another department


### Hunter

Items can also be linked to a read-in note via Hunter


### Tracking

Read-in notes track the way they have been linked to other elements in the system via the hunter, as well as when a user has copied a read-in note from another read-in note.


### Communication

Read-in notes include a chat section for collaboration.

You can send editorial alerts from Read-in notes. Great to send a Read-in Note to your department

You can also follow Read-in notes to track their activity via the activity feeds and notifications.


### Activity Log

Logs also contain activity read-in notes to check the change history of them and be able to navigate to the latest activity
