### LOGS (READ-IN NOTES):


#### Intro

- Logs: Are like a sibling to a story or an episode that allows you to gather some information in a text block, add attachments with a board, share it with your teams

- This can be used for internal information sharing, morning read-ins and can really help with the onboarding and communication process.


#### Quick Add


- Logs can be created from the quick add menu

As opposed to Stories, the Description field will be mapped to its Notes Section.


Logs can be private. Simply select the option when you create them. Useful for private briefings until you are ready to share with a department.


#### Categorisation

- Logs are not a content creation project, as such Logs cannot be turned into stories or productions as they are unique. However a linked story, pitch, event or log can be created out of them

- A log cannot be shared with other departments but they can be copied to another department


#### Hunter

- Items can also be linked to a log via Hunter


#### Communication

- Logs include a chat section for collaboration.

- You can send editorial alerts from Logs. Great to send a Read-in Note to your department

- You can also follow Logs to track their activity via the activity feeds and notifications.
