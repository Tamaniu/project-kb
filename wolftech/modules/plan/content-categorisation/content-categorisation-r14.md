## CONTENT CREATION AND CATEGORISATION

### CREATING CONTENT OUT OF PLANNING SOURCES
Content can start from Feeds or External Calendars as seen in the research section. Go to one of those sources and use the Quick add button or drag those sources to a Story Column to create it.

Content can be created out of internal events if you are planning events forward before deciding if content will be made out of it. This will copy its title, description, attachments in the board, and location.

To do so go to the event and click on the create X from event

It will also offer you the option to link its bookings, which may be useful for associated guests for example.

### TRANSITIONING CONTENT (PITCH TO CONTENT)
Content can be transitioned from a Pitch to a Story or Episode.
Again, the concept here is that all information added at the pitch stage is now available when converted to a story

### QUICK ADD

Content can start from scratch from the Quick Add.
Here you can create a pitch, a story, an event, an episode (if add on is available), or a read-in note

The basic information form includes adding a title, a description (which can be richly formatted text), Genre(s), a Department and a date and a production type (For additional information).

Depending on the type of content you may have more or less options.
For example, events may have options to set start and end times and time zone, and episodes may have options for production templates.

Fields that are mandatory at this stage will have a red dot next to them.

As you enter the title and description Wolftech will do a system check to identify if similar content already exists in the organization. We call this “Find Similar”. This is to prevent the unnecessary creation of duplicated content. If you find another department is working on this story already you can simply share it with your department to start collaborating with them.

The find similar search can be simplified to be only titled base, otherwise it is looking across title, description and additional information searchable fields.

Once a story or production has been created you can categorize it in diverse ways to make sure the right people can find it later. All project categories enable others to filter searches or plan views to display just the right content they are after.
Categories include departments, offices, dates, deadlines, uniqueIDs, genres-Tags, or Production types (Formats). The latter will also define any custom metadata forms for your project.

### TEAM CATEGORIES
Who is working on a project determines who gets to access the project, so it is of utmost importance.

##### Private vs Public Project

A project may start as private, meaning only the person who creates it sees it. To make it private you can do so in the quick add or after project creation

From there that person can give access to named collaborators via the story or episode permissions under the 3-dot menu

Note, when converting a private pitch to a story or episode you will be asked if you want to keep it private. There is an exception to this when you have the “Enable permission inheritance when converting note to story” admin option enabled, meaning the conversion of a pitch to a story will automatically propagate the correct new permissions for the story.

##### Department(s) and Office(s)

Once the right privacy level has been set you can choose which department, and office will be working on your project.

These are 2 of the most important categories in the system as they will make your story visible to your colleagues.

For efficiency, the project main department is auto selected by your default department.
The same goes for the office This can be set in my page profile:
When you use the Quick Add function to create a story you will default to your home department, but you will also be able to start with a shared story with other departments:

Once the main department and office have been auto selected you can share or unshare your stories with other departments or offices.
To share it with another department, go to the 3-dot menu:
You can search the departments (including hierarchy folders) to select who should collaborate on the story
Do note Productions cannot be shared with other departments. Departments collaborate on stories and a particular department or brand oversees a long form episode that may be linked to the story.

Once a story is shared across multiple departments it is easy to recognize it because it will have their colors on the story sideline as well as you will be able to see them when hovering over the department button
Sharing a story is also possible via the plan view.

Adding a story to multiple offices is also easy by clicking on the office button and searching and selecting other offices
This is possible both for stories and productions.

##### Pitch vs Department Access

It is also possible to define 2 levels of access based on a subset development user group having access to pitches in a department and the whole department having access to the story or production once it has been converted from a pitch. To determine this split-level access, check the admin section.
### TIMED-BASED CATEGORIES

Defining when a story or production happens determines whether those projects are shown or hidden from planning calendar views in the left menu or in the plan view, so it is critical to get it right. There are multiple options to manage these so let´s look at them.

##### Story Time Categories

The next categorization is all about when the story is happening. A story can happen
- on one specific day
- be a multi-day story
- Be a timeless (evergreen story)

To make it extremely fast to create a breaking story the default date will be today´s date. To define a different date when a story is happening simply click on the date picker. Select a date, add multiple dates, or mark the story as timeless.

##### Production Time Categories

A Production is by default a project that typically spans over longer periods of time, so the options are as follows
- Timeless Production (Evergreen) this is its initial status until a schedule is created)
- Regular Production (Once a “from and to” time is added)
- Deadline (Available to both timeless and regular productions to indicate estimated delivery date.

On the header what you see is the project deadline, which is manually entered.

But on any planning views. the home page or the timeline page you will be able to see the from and to time

Setting the dates of a production is done in the timeline tab.
Click on the date picker below the name.
To speed things up, the default will be starting on today´s date.
If a different option is needed, then set either the start or the end date for your production and your project template will automate the timeline for you.
Note how you can also set the deadline here although that is also possible from the header.
### EDITORIAL TAGS

Content tags allow to organize

##### Genre(s) (Tags)

Genres are a way to categorize the projects that are being created and help organizations align their output to their editorial and publishing strategy.

Genres can be mandatory before greenlighting. Check with your admin

Search and select genre(s) Based on Your project content.

If multiple genres are selected it is easy to identify them with the hover over:
We are working on AI capabilities to help you add tags and genres quickly based on your content title and description - Check with us soon!

##### Production Types (Formats)

Describing the format of your project allows others to filter all content output by its Format which can help with discovery and distribution of your content to more places. The format may also help you with the hiring process to find the crew with the relevant skills.

Some examples of formats may include

Art, Animation, Branded, BTS, Commercial, Documentary (>1h), Editorial, Experiential, Feature Documentary, Feature Film, Game Show, Illustration, Investigative, Live Events, Live Music, Live TV, Long Form, Music Video, Narrative, News. Podcast, Pre-Roll, Reels, Social, Stills, Scripted Television. Unscripted Television. Variety, VFX. VR/AR/XR, Written Editorial
This may also be used by editorial teams to define their coverage types into
Editorial Dayside
Editorial Breaking
Editorial Nightside
Editorial Overnight

At Wolftech we have made it easier to select your formats with a quick search bar

Formats can also be set at the publishing point level so if a story is always editorial but the output of it may be better categorized into formats that is also possible.

At wolftech we have gone a step further with formats to allow you to have custom metadata forms based on the format of your project because we know that there is always more pertinent information that you may want to capture depending on the format of your project. We call them custom fields

##### Additional Information (Custom Fields)

Depending on the type of project you may require more information than a title and a description to correctly describe your project or help your team support you along the way.
So, depending on the format you choose you may or may not be presented with some fields in the additional information section.

Fields marked with a red dot indicate they are mandatory before the project can be greenlit. This is a way to enforce a basic amount of information before the project can go forward.

In these cases, we recommend grouping those into a specific tab, so it is easy to find them at project start:

You can then set other tabs for other useful information that may not be mandatory but more operational

As you can make some of these fields searchable and filterable these become immensely powerful when combined.

##### Content Groups

When multiple projects, stories, productions, pitches, and events are related but are not part of the same department or office or genre or format you may decide to create a group. This is particularly useful for large events like The Olympics, or Elections.

For this simply use the 3-dot menu - add to group option and those managing the group content output will now be able to see your project as part of it.

### ADMIN

##### Admin Departments Access

Departments can be administered in the admin - Organizational Levels section. Only Admins can do so.
On the left side you can add any levels of departments and subdepartments.
Here you can set which user groups have access to the Stories in Content Permissions,
To enable the option to have different permissions level at pitch or story level you can do so in the customer options section
You can then determine the user groups that can see the pitches independently in the organizational levels:
If no user group is defined in the note's permissions, it is assumed everyone in the content permissions group can access all pitches too.

For more info on other options in this panel see the admin guide.

##### Admin Offices

Offices can be admined in the admin - Offices section. Only Admins can do so
Note, you can mark an office to be booking only to apply only to people rather than project categorization.

##### Admin Genres

Genre(s) can be admined in the admin - Offices section. Only Admins can do so
In the customer option menu, you can also make them mandatory before greenlighting
##### Admin Production Types (Formats)

Working with production types is optional for the customer. So, to use Production types first enable it in the customer option menu:
To define your Production Types (Formats) simply go to the admin - Production Types section and add or rename as needed

Formats can set at the story-production level to define the format of your story or the publishing point level, to define the format of your output from the story

  - Publishing Points (Based on publishing formats)
  - The finance section

Once a tab exists you can begin adding custom fields to that tab

In this section you can also choose to include the location-time summary if wanted or add a new field

Choose from one of the existing fields or add a new one.
Here you will also be able to determine if it is mandatory for greenlight
Whether it is searchable, and in the case of single or multi select whether it can be filterable in searches.

Those will then be available in the global search function:
##### Admin Content Groups

Groups are admined through the left menu. Everyone can admin groups
It is easy to organize them into folders, archive them or add them to your groups.

##### Admin IDs and layout

In the admin layout section, you can define which IDs are visible to the users. Whether to show the internal ID or the GUID, whether to show the budget code and in the case of productions whether to show the ShowID as well.
