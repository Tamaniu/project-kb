## GREENLIGHT


### GREENLIGHT

####### Intro

Greenlighting a story or production is sometimes required and can be done by a subset of people with those correct permissions. The process of greenlight can be as simple or involved as admins decide to make it.

The greenlight process can have technical checks as well as organisational operational checks that are not defined in the system.

Generally the production greenlight process is more involved than the story greenlight process.

Let's look at the options.

####### Operational Greenlight Checks

Beyond the content categorisation where you can define which elements are required before greenlight including genres, formats, descriptions, the correct department level or additional information, optionally you may enforce other checks like:

- Adding at least one publishing point to know where you plan to publish the content

- Going through a budget check

- Adding a budget code

- Deciding on which optional stages are required for the production

- Reviewing the timeline

- Creating the Story-Production MAM collection

Once you are happy with the project readiness if you have the permissions you will be able to greenlight it

####### Greenlight notification and Summary

Once you click on the greenlight button it will mark the project as greenlit and will notify all booked resources and defined greenlight contacts for the department that the production is now greenlit.

It will also send a greenlight summary email to all people booked on it. (Note this is currently disabled as we are fixing the formatting of it.)

####### Greenlight Notifications and summary

Other granular requirements like adding a publishing point is done with customisation support. Contact the wolftech team if needed.


In the admin notifications settings you will be able to define which options the users will have for this type of notification and whether they can unsubscribe to them. Typical greenlight emails are enforced at admin level to make sure everyone is on the same page on project start.


For each department you can also set a number of users that should receive notification of greenlight even though they are not directly booked on the story or production. This is useful for execs that are just overseeing a lot of content and may not be booked on the projects
