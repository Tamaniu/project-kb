### GUIDANCE ON MOBILE

#### Intro:
Wolftech Go makes you work seamlessly with the Wolftech News web client and all of your colleagues. Please regard that the new mobile application only works together with Wolftech version 11.
NB! This guidance for the new mobile app  is still under development. Both the version with the old feeds view and the new feeds view.
The Wolftech Go application gives you the opportunity to work efficiently in the field and when you are away from the office. It syncs a lot faster with the web browser and will have the same functionality.
One of the advantages is that you can book a resource in the web client. Then the reporter or photographer would immediately get a notification on the mobile app and confirm or decline the task.

The new functionality in the Wolftech News app would be useful to:
Search for Employee/Freelance contacts and directly contact them
See the tasks you are booked on
Be notified on your phone when someone assigns you a task
Check Newswires-Feeds directly on the app
See Risk Assessments for your location
Get directions to Points of Interests or Locations

### CREATE A STORY
#### +-button

As in Wolftech web clients push the orange +-button to create a Story, Note, Event, Log or to send an Alert.
Once you have created a story you will see that some fields are pre-filled, while others you can fill in yourself like in the Description field.

#### Inside a story

Inside a story you will recognize most of the options from the Wolftech News web client. Starting up on the right side side you will see the chat icon which allows you to send messages to your team and others who are attached to the story.

#### Story - 3 dot menu

The 3 dot icon will give you several options to categorize what kind of story this is. In the pop up menu you can also follow a story, send alerts or add to a group.

Story information

To get more information about the story you push the arrow symbol at the right side of the story window. The page will then expand and a field giving you information about Department, Office and Production type will appear.

#### Story - Booking

When you need information about technical and human resources booked on your story, you simply press Bookings. You will immediately see the resource booked and if they have confirmed the assignment.

#### Story -  Publishing points

Choosing Publishing points symbol you will get to see the published destination of a story. The story above has been published to X/Twitter and the 24/7 News Channel at 2 PM. If you click at the +-symbol up to the right, you will be able to add a Publishing point.

When adding a publishing point, you must fill in Name, and select the destination from the Publishing point folder which gives you a lot of publishing points options as shown in the window to the right .

#### Story -  Enter a Publishing points

When you enter a story in the Publishing points section you will get to a new window showing you more options, like Bookings, Description, Additional information, Activity and Note.

#### Story -  Publishing points 3 dot menu

#### Story -  Publishing point Status

The purple status icon where the red arrow points, will show you the status on the publishing points. You can change this by pressing til icon and then set a new status as shown above. The editorial team will then easily see the status on the story.

The top meny show the options and you can define and set name on the tabs. The benefit is that you by this quickly can get an overview of your production.

#### Story - Description

To gain insight of what the story contains you do the same as in Wolftech News web client and simply press the Description button.

Background information - Editorial and chat

Like Bloomberg, NBC News and CNN. In this window US Twitter/X stories are shown as one of the feed views.

For choosing the different feed views simply click on the 3 dot menu up on the right, and a pop up window will appear. Choose the feed view you need for your work.

By pressing on a Story you will get more information about the content. You can create a story and get to the main source.

Go to source  will take you to a third party source like in this case, a Twitter/X-story from Bloomberg. From the X-platform you can go to the original story at Bloomberg. It works the same way if you are in a story from a news agency, a news organization, Dataminr, RSS or social media sites like X, Facebook, Instagram.

### ALERTS

From the +-button you will be able to send alerts. This is a simple way to make sure your organization, or part of it is aware of an event occurring. Alerts can show in Feeds or be sent by email and notifications.
In the communication bar you can select if you want to receive notifications for alerts. Do note: everyone receives critical alerts despite this setting. This enables users following non critical alerts to be aware of them.

#### Create new alert

From here you will have the option  to create alerts which are not attached to a specific item in the system. Simply select the template you want to use and fill it out. Go to the Admin Configuration in the web browser to create new templates. Push the red button to send the alert.

#### Critical Alerts

Everyone in the organization receives critical alerts. This enables users following non critical alerts to be aware of them.

### Email and Feed Alerts

You can send an alert as an email or as an internal Feed. It will then appear in your feed view.
You can define who you would like to send the emails too, and use email groups.
It works the same way in Feed Alerts. You can define a user group or individual users to receive alerts.

### PLAN VIEW/STORY VIEW

When you want to go to the story view, just press the hamburger button at the bottom of the menu (highlighted). Choose the Stories tab which will take you to the new views.

#### Story view

When you get inside the story view you can swipe to the next view which in this case is the Event view. The blue hamburger icon will give you shortcuts to go straight into the same view as when you swipe.

#### Shortcuts

Instead of swiping between the different views, you can simply push the blue hamburger icon and choose the buttons which quickly take you to the view that you want to work in.

#### Story - filters

If you press the filter button (red circle) you will be able to select the brands that you want to show in your story. If you don´t choose any of this, all the brands will be visible in the story.
With this function you can select what kind of content you would like to see and filter Offices, Genres, Production types, Content types and statuses and Groups.

#### Publishing points

When you push the Publish button you will see the publishing points that you have pre-selected from the filter list up on the right side.

#### Publishing, sub platforms

When pushing the filter button you will get into sub platforms. The publishing points can be selected from the list above.

#### Timeless publish

Timeless publish will guide you into the stories that you have stored in the left view in the Wolftech News web client. From this site on the mobile app you will have the overview over the timeless stories that you might want to publish to one of your shows. Pushing the filter button you will see the same list and then choose what publishing points you want to see in the view.

#### Events

This will give you an overview of all the events that you will find  in Wolftech News. The blue hamburger icon will give you shortcut buttons to Logs, Event and Stories view.

#### Event filters

Pushing the filter button in Events you will find different fields where you fill in metadata to customize the event view.

#### Logs

Choosing the Logs button you will be able to see logs from different shows and other productions in your company. Logs is a simple version of a story. It could be used to evaluate a news show, summarize the daily production or to set up a plan for the production the next days.

#### Groups

There are a lot of options in the Group section. A user group is a means of grouping multiple users for the purpose of giving them specific authorisation permissions, access to content, access to specific plans or feeds views or allow users to communicate directly with the entire user group with an @mention handle. Giving your different functional teams some permissions and categorisations is the first part to set up the right collaboration workflows for your organization. Note that you must create a new group in the Wolftech News web client.

#### My Groups

You can set up your group favorites in My groups. Note that you have to organize this in the Wolftech News web client.

To follow a story or production simply hit the follow button in the web browser:

#### Notifications

Once you follow a story or an episode you will get a notification on your mobile phone when someone changes the story. This will keep you updated when you are on location.

#### Activity

To be updated on a Story that you follow,  go to the hamburger icon on the right side (marked in red) and then the Activity window will open.

#### Activity

In the mobile app this will reveal all of the latest activity on a story or episode. You will also see who updated it.

#### Unfollow

To unfollow an individual story just click on the notification and go to the 3 dot menu. This will direct you to the story and give you the option to unfollow it.

#### Unfollow story and Unfollow all

Once you unfollow a story it will update in the web client and in the app and you will no longer receive  notifications. As you can see above there are several other options in this pop up window.
If you want to unfollow all stories, simply push the 3 dot menu and then a pop up tab gives you the options to Unfollow all.

### BOOKING

Booking in Wolftech News web client

Book any resource or equipment you would like from the web browser. Go into the actual Story and choose the Bookings-button. Then decide whether it is a Technical, Human or a Team resource.

When you have sent the request, the resource will appear in the Story and you can follow the status field (the blue box) and wait for the resource to confirm or decline the task..

#### Booking in Wolftech Go Mobile app
The mobile app gives you the same options to book resources as Contacts, Staffs, Guests, Teams and Technical as in the web client.

From the Home page you can open a Story and then you will see the Bookings tab on the top.

#### Story Bookings

By clicking on the +-symbol you will be able to book your resource directly in the mobile app.

In the story bookings you will have three options. Add contact, Add booking and Add request.

From here you can book Staff or Guests just like in the Wolftech News web client. You can book both in the Story, Locations and Pub points.

If you press the Bookings button you will have three options, Staff, Technical, Human and Team.
Choosing any of these you can book any resource you want in your organization.

#### How to book a resource

If you would like to assign a resource, just choose from the menu and then you will have a lot of options from your organization.

When you have decided who you want to assign for the task, then push the name to send a request or to book her or him.

You can send a request for the resource that you need in the production and you can do the same in a Story, Locations or an important Pub points.

### Booking Notifications

When you have booked or requested  a resource, reporters, photographers, guests, technical staff or the members of a team will get the assigned request immediately on their mobile phone as a notification.

#### Confirm a booking

Once you have requested a human resource, the reporter or a photographer who is on location, would be able to confirm the task within seconds after they received the notifications. Just push the blue tab to confirm.

#### Request

When the resource has confirmed the task, a green tab marked as booked will appear in the mobile app window and in the web client. The resource is now connected to the story and will be visible for the team and any others in the organization that has access to the story. When you're in a story you can get more information about the bookings and publishing points.

### CHAT

Inside a story you can go to the chat icon to send a message or to start a chat. It will save you a lot of time to communicate with your colleagues in the actual story. In that way you do not have to use other communication channels to give important information which is only relevant for your team.

#### Message

The chat function and the options to send messages in Wolftech News is a good opportunity to change your communication and to make your workflow more efficient.. A benefit is that all your communication can take place in a story that you and your colleagues are attached to, so that no one misses important information.

When you are in the Messages window, simply start writing a message at the bottom of the screen. This will appear in the story, both in the web browser and the mobile app.
