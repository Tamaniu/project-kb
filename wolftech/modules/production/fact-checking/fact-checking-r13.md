## FACT CHECKING AND CONTENT AUTHENTICITY
We have a range of tools destined to safeguard your content authenticity and trust in an era of AI generated content and fake news.
### FACTIVERSE CLAIM VERIFICATION
Factiverse continues to be available (with separate license: Factiverse.ai) to fact check text from your background information segments and provide sources for or against your claims
This module is available via the Editorial section:
Once loaded it will analyze the content to extract claims that are then researched online to find supporting and refuting sources.
From there you can choose to trust those sources or not, but it will save you considerable time in this fact-finding exercise
### C2PA: CONTENT PROVENANCE
We have partnered with the Content Authenticity Alliance to offer workflows to determine the content provenance of the files added to the board.
Content Credentials are currently supported across image, video, and audio files. Not live streams. It is an industry standard that is being implemented by camera manufacturers, image and video manipulation software and publishing platforms so you can show to your audience the content provenance of your media from acquisition to distribution to significantly increase audience trust.
Files added to the board will have a CR watermark to display its content credentials.
If the files have any content provenance credentials, we will display them on hover over the CR watermark
From it you can see the last change done to this file and you have an option to inspect the element. This will open the file on the https://contentcredentials.org/ website to check its full history.
