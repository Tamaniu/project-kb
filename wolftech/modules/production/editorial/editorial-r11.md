## BACKGROUND INFORMATION - EDITORIAL
### DEVELOPMENT (EDITORIAL BACKGROUND INFORMATION)
Intro
In a pitch phase or in a development phase you will write a one liner, outline, plus do research and develop your script with interviews, references etc. The Background information section is built to help you deal with these tasks leveraging multiple text editors.
Each tab allows you to develop different aspects of your story-production.
Overall, your admin can decide the required tabs for your organization and users have a full text editor where you can style your document as you would expect from any other text editor.
The Text Editor
Each Text Block is equivalent to a document with full styling capabilities:
To ease your navigation the bottom editor will always be sticky and visible no matter the length of your segment so you are always in control.
Copying a text from another source will retain most of its styling while keeping it within the framework supported by the system for onwards publishing workflows:
If you try to edit a segment of text edited by another person you may get a warning message This is to prevent users unexpectedly editing important newsgathering items that may have been written by other colleagues:
Attaching Board items
For each segment you are able to attach items either by pasting them into the text or by attaching items that may already be in your story board.
If linking from board you will be able to do bulk selections::
Items added to a segment can also be detached from the segment after the fact:
Any images included in an editorial segment will be saved in the board and you will be able to see which editorial segment they are related to with a hyperlink for ease of navigation:
Links (unfurling)
Links added to a segment of text will display as hyperlinks
If you hover over a link in the document you will be able to see the preview of the link:
A link can be copied, opened or turned into a link card with the right click option:
Link cards display at the bottom for a more visual experience from where it can also be deleted or added to the board:
Fact Checking and Labels
You can do fact checking of both editorial segments and the script leveraging Factiverse as well as requesting internal guidance (Legal, etc) to help you determine you can cover those elements of the story
With Factiverse you are always in control of your decisions. You can check the sources that support your claims or refute it so you can always decide whether you trust those sources.
Whether assisted with AI or manually checked Fact checking can help your team mark your different text segments as reportable to help you build your story:
Marking it as important
A segment can also be marked important editorially to highlight it. This will give it a yellow outline and tag it with important:
Guidance
As you add information you will see you can navigate down through multiple segments and wolftech will make the editing bar sticky at the top so you are always able to edit the text.
If at any point you require guidance on any of those segments you can request it. See the Guidance Guide for more information.
Filtering Segments
You will be able to filter all of your research at the top by these statuses, its importance nature of whether attachments are present from the top
Summarisation
If your story becomes fairly big, note some of them we have seen to grow to 200+ segments, you may want to use our AI summarisation assistant to get up to date with the latest status of the story
This will summarize all of the segments for you.
Following changes
If you want to subscribe to any changes to a particular segment you can do so by clicking on the Notify as modified. This will make sure any changes to this particular text box will trigger a notification to you.
Sharing an Editorial Segment
It is easy to share an editorial segment by getting the link to it from the 3 dot menu.
Publishing Segments
Segments can be leveraged for publishing. On your publishing point template, if you have a multi line field you will be able to import content:
When you click on it you can navigate to select a background information item to import:
Simply add it and then edit it from your template before publishing:
### USE CASES
Editorial Segments
These can be used to import newsgathering in support of your story., all research or development of the story can be added here.
Interviews-Transcripts
You will also be able to leverage AI tools on to transform the text in those editorial segments.
This could be useful for summarisation, or enquiring the content of an interview transcript with a custom prompt.
Add the script of a transcript to the transcript tab:
Then use the custom prompts for asking questions about content in the interview transcript:
Script:
For Scene Beats or full scripts.
Use the add text field option to break each scene beat
Access
For description of characters and access it is useful to have a separate tab.
This may be also used for location access, etc.
Stills
For your plans to shoot promotional stills on set as well as your thumbnails. Simply attach pictures to this section and add comments.
Music:
Drop here links to music or guidance on style of music required. This may also be useful to get rights and clearance guidance on the music.
Once a track is cleared it would be easy to ingest the item into the media storage for use in the edit.
Visual References
Many times it is useful to add visual reference for the shoot as well as a shooting guide or bible to help your team deliver to your vision.
Logistics
For any development requests and descriptions on transportation and logistical requirements
